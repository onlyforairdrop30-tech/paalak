Convenient Location & Transport

Samarpan Campus is centrally situated in Sector-28, Gandhinagar, right at Samarpan Circle (KH-7 Road). It enjoys direct connectivity from all sectors of Gandhinagar, Ahmedabad (via SG Highway / Gandhinagar Highway), and surrounding towns.

🚌 By Bus:
Direct GSRTC & local municipal city buses available from Gandhinagar Central Bus Station (Pathika) to Sector-28 / Samarpan Circle.
🚆 By Train / Metro:
Close to Gandhinagar Capital Railway Station and Ahmedabad-Gandhinagar Metro connectivity corridor.
🚗 By Private Vehicle:
Ample student and visitor parking available on the campus premises with smooth KH-7 road access.
