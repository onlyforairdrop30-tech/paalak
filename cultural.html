Cultural Events & Celebrations

Celebrating Gujarati heritage, artistic expression, festive unity, and student creativity at Samarpan Campus.

Home / Cultural Events
Tradition & Joy

Navratri Garba Mahotsav

Navratri is celebrated with great enthusiasm at Samarpan Institute of Computer Science. Students participate in traditional Garba, cultural performances, and festive activities, creating an atmosphere of joy, unity, and cultural pride.

Dressed in vibrant traditional attire (Chaniya Choli and Kurta), students and faculty join together to dance to rhythmic folk music. Prizes for Best Attire and Best Garba Dancer add excitement to the festive night.

Innovation & Teamwork

Annual Food Festival

The Food Festival at Samarpan Institute of Computer Science is a vibrant event where students showcase their culinary talents, creativity, and teamwork.

Various student-run food stalls offer delicious homemade regional delicacies and innovative street foods. The event creates a fun-filled atmosphere while encouraging budget management, marketing, and real-life entrepreneurship skills.

Talent & Recognition

Samarpan Annual Day Celebration

The Annual Function is one of the most awaited events at Samarpan Institute of Computer Science. Students enthusiastically participate in cultural performances, dance, music, drama, and various creative activities.

The event provides a platform for students to showcase their stage talents, build public speaking confidence, and celebrate academic and extra-curricular achievements together with faculty members, trustees, and distinguished guests.
