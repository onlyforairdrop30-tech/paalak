Core Facilities for Student Success

Comprehensive infrastructure engineered to provide optimal learning, research, recreation, and personal safety.

Facility 1

Advanced Computer Laboratories

Our Computer Lab is a state-of-the-art facility designed to support student learning, foster innovation, and facilitate hands-on experience in computing and computer applications. A computer lab is a crucial component of our BCA program, serving as a dedicated space to facilitate practical learning and programming projects.

Facility 2

Spacious Smart Classrooms

The spacious and open design of our classrooms reflects our commitment to creating an effective learning environment. By emphasizing collaboration, comfort, and access to modern audio-visual technology, we empower students to engage fully in their lectures and prepare for future challenges.

Facility 3

Central Knowledge Library

The library is an essential resource supporting the academic mission of our institution. By offering access to a diverse collection of textbooks, research journals, reference volumes, magazines, and digital e-resources, the library fosters an inspiring environment of research, study, and intellectual growth.

Facility 4

Sports Ground & Horse Riding Club

Having a large sports ground and access to sporting instruments is fundamental to providing students with an enriching lifestyle. These facilities promote physical fitness, teamwork, sportsmanship, and personal growth while nurturing community spirit.

Facility 5

Hygienic Campus Canteen

Having an on-campus canteen is a vital amenity for students and faculty. The canteen offers fresh, hygienic food and refreshments at student-friendly prices, providing a lively social space for relaxation and friendship between classes.

Facility 6

Campus ATM & Green Surroundings

On-campus 24x7 ATM banking facilities enhance the convenience of students and staff. The campus features lush gardens, mature trees, and pedestrian-friendly walkways that create an eco-friendly and pollution-free study sanctuary.
