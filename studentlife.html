Campus Life at Samarpan

Campus life at Samarpan Institute of Computer Science is filled with learning, friendship, teamwork, and memorable experiences. Students participate in various activities that help them grow academically and personally while creating lasting memories.

Beyond laboratory coding and lectures, our students engage in study groups, technical forums, cultural showcases, and outdoor sports in an inclusive and encouraging environment.

🌱 Balanced Development

We nurture not just technical coding skills, but emotional resilience, communication, and leadership.

Athletics & Fitness

Games Day & Sports Meets

Games Day is one of the most exciting events at Samarpan Institute of Computer Science. Students enthusiastically participate in indoor and outdoor games that promote teamwork, sportsmanship, leadership, and healthy competition.

The annual sports festival creates a fun-filled atmosphere and strengthens friendships among students. Events include cricket tournaments, volleyball, badminton, chess, carrom, table tennis, and athletics.

🏆 Sportsmanship & Physical Health

Our expansive playground offers dedicated tracks, courts, and equestrian training opportunities.
